How the data and formulas work

Pipeline: CSV fixtures → validated SQLite import → SQL queries → this portable report. Money is stored as integer cents. Foreign keys, unique keys, and validation reject inconsistent inputs; a failed import rolls back the entire replacement.

CAC: All acquisition costs represented in this demo divided by unique new customers acquired during the window. Other costs are fictional allocations. They do not establish a company's full cost of acquisition.

ROAS: Gross campaign-attributed order revenue during the window divided by ad spend during that window. Repeat purchases count toward revenue but not new-customer counts. Each sample order has one campaign attribution, so channels do not overlap.

Unavailable values: A zero denominator displays “—”, rather than an invented zero ratio. Real connectors would also need reconciliation of attribution windows, time zones, currencies, refunds, and account permissions.

Alerts: Deterministic rules describe changes in the sample data. They do not diagnose causes or make changes to advertising budgets.
